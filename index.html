About Us

A Premier Institution for Holistic Education

Creative Public School has been a cornerstone of quality education in our community since 2005. We offer a nurturing environment where students grow academically, emotionally, and socially.

  • Comprehensive curriculum from Nursery to Class 10th following CBSE guidelines
  • State-of-the-art smart classrooms with interactive digital boards
  • Highly qualified, experienced, and dedicated teaching faculty
  • Safe, secure, and child-friendly campus environment
  • Vibrant sports programs, cultural activities & annual fests
  • Modern computer lab with internet & programming courses
  • Well-stocked library with 10,000+ books and digital resources
  • School transport facility covering major routes in the city

Our Facilities

World-Class Facilities

We provide the best infrastructure and resources to ensure a holistic learning experience for every student.

Smart Classrooms

Interactive digital boards, projectors, and multimedia learning tools in every classroom for engaging lessons.

Computer Lab

Modern computer lab with 60+ systems, high-speed internet, and qualified instructors for digital literacy.

Library

A well-stocked library with 10,000+ books, e-books, journals, and a dedicated reading area for students.

Playground

Spacious ground for cricket, football, athletics and multiple outdoor sports to promote physical fitness.

Transport Facility

Safe and comfortable school buses covering all major routes, with GPS tracking and trained drivers.

Science Lab

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for hands-on experiments and scientific exploration.
